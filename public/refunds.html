Refund Policy

Last updated: 02-11-2026

1. How refunds work

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for all Costify3D orders, so refunds are processed by Paddle on our behalf, back to your original payment method.

2. Our refund window

  • Pro (one-time): you can request a refund within 14 days of purchase.
  • Master (subscription): you can request a refund within 14 days of your first payment, and within 5 days of a renewal payment if you did not intend to renew.

After that window, we may still refund you at our discretion, for example for a technical problem we cannot fix.

3. Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period.

4. How to ask

Use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or visit paddle.net, or email us at mtruvius@gmail.com with your order number and purchase email. We will usually respond within 2 business days.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect your statutory consumer rights, including if the product is faulty or not as described.
